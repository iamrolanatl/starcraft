# Credits

## Theme skins
All four alternate skins (Blockcraft, Brick City, Battle Island, Kart Rush) are **original, CSS-generated art** (gradients and
patterns in `src/app/globals.css`). They are inspired by popular kids' game aesthetics but use no logos, trademarks, game fonts,
screenshots or ripped assets, and no game names appear in the UI.
